Privacy Policy for Robot - Game App

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information, permissions, and payments to provide you with an interactive Robot - Game App experience.

1. Information Collection and Use

Robot - Game App is designed to prioritize user privacy We do not collect, store, or sell your personal identification information (such as your name or address) on our own servers.

The application accesses certain device features locally to provide its core functionality. This data is processed in real-time on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Calendar Information

The application may request access to your device's calendar so the Robot can display your scheduled events. This data stays on your phone.

Activity Recognition

The application may use the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission to detect relevant user activity so that the Robot can respond or behave accordingly. Activity information is used for the app's functionality and is not transmitted to our servers.

Body Sensors

The application may use the BODY_SENSORS permission to access supported sensor information when required for the Robot's functionality. Sensor information is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted to our servers.

System Date and Time

The application accesses the device's system date and time to display the correct date and time within the application.

2. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

Robot Game App does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or cloud-based services to collect user information.

Information accessed through Calendar, Activity Recognition, or Body Sensors permissions is used for the functionality of the application and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Permissions

Robot Game App may request the following permissions, depending on the features available in the application:

  • Calendar: Used to access calendar events and appointments so the Robot can display or respond to scheduled events.
  • Activity Recognition: Used to detect relevant physical activity so the Robot can respond accordingly.
  • Body Sensors: Used to access supported body sensor information when required for the application's functionality.
  • System Date and Time: Used to display the correct date and time.

The application does not intentionally access location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. Because Robot Game App does not transmit users' personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side database containing users' personal information.

Users can control or revoke application permissions through the permission settings available on their Wear OS device.

5.Subscriptions and Financial Data

Robot - Game App offers a "Pro" version via Google Play Subscriptions. Payment Processing: All financial transactions, including subscriptions and cancellations, are handled exclusively by Google Play Billing. Data Access: We do not have access to your credit card details, bank information, or billing address.

6. Children's Privacy

Robot Game App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because the application does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, we do not knowingly maintain personal information belonging to children.

7. Third-Party Services

Robot Game App does not currently use third-party advertising, analytics, or cloud data-storage services to collect personal information.

The application may interact with services or functionality provided by the Android operating system, such as the device calendar and sensors. These services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application or its features.

When we make changes, we will update the Effective Date shown at the top of this Privacy Policy. We encourage users to review this page periodically for any updates.

9. Disclaimer

Robot Game App features may change or be updated over time. The application is provided for its intended functionality, and users should review the permissions requested by the application and the available device settings.
